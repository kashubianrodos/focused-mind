# App Store review notes

The block below is the **Notes** field of *App Review Information* in App
Store Connect (plain text, 4,000 characters at most). It answers the six
items App Review asked for under Guideline 2.1 (Information Needed). Replace
`<VIDEO_URL>` before pushing it.

```
1. SCREEN RECORDING
A recording made on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS, starting from launching the app and showing the typical flow with a real headband: <VIDEO_URL>
The app has no account registration, login, user-generated content or paid content, so none of these appear in it.

2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Focused Relaxed Mind is a neurofeedback app for adults who own a BrainBit-family consumer EEG headband (Mindo, BrainBit, BrainBit 2) and want to train focus or relaxation. It reads the EEG over Bluetooth, shows a focus score (or a calm score in meditation sessions) every second, and plays a gentle soundscape (birds, ocean or forest) that guides the user back when focus drifts or deepens as they relax. It solves the problem that people can't tell how focused or calm they are while working or meditating; the app turns brain rhythms into simple, immediate feedback and a session history.

3. HOW TO USE THE APP (no login, no sample files needed)
- Without a headband: on the Focus tab tap Scan. "Simulated headband" is always listed (also when Bluetooth is off or not allowed). Tap it to connect.
- With a headband: switch it on, allow Bluetooth when asked, tap Scan and pick the band.
- Choose Focus or Meditation and a length (25, 50, 90 min or open-ended), then tap Start session. A short signal check and calibration run first, then the live score, the relaxation-concentration scale, brain rhythms and the soundscape (turn the sound on).
- End the session to see the summary. History lists past sessions; each can be exported as a CSV file (Files > On My iPhone > Focused Mind).
- Settings: soundscape, feedback mode, volume and trigger delay.
The simulated headband produces EEG-like data that goes through the same scoring, feedback and session code as a real band; its focus holds for about a minute, then drifts so the feedback visibly changes.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app makes no network requests and uses no servers, accounts, analytics, ads, payment, AI or data providers. All processing runs on the device with BrainBit's on-device SDKs (neurosdk2 for the Bluetooth connection, em_st_artifacts for the focus estimate), both MIT-licensed and bundled in the app. Data is stored only on the device.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in every storefront where it is offered (39 European countries); features and content do not vary by region.

6. REGULATION AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
The app is a wellness tool, not a medical device: it does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition, and it says so on first launch and in Settings > About. It collects no health data off the device.
Third-party material: the forest soundscape is "Daytime Wind with Birds and Forest Grass" from Pixabay, used under the Pixabay Content License (commercial use in apps allowed, no attribution required): https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/ . The birds and ocean soundscapes are generated by the developer. The BrainBit SDKs are MIT-licensed (copyright BrainBit). The App Store screenshots come from real sessions with a Mindo headband.

Privacy policy: https://kashubianrodos.github.io/focused-mind/privacy.html
```

## Recording script

Record on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS, with **Control Center ›
Screen Recording** (microphone off). Turn on Do Not Disturb first so no
notifications appear in the video. The video is
about 2–3 minutes, with sound on so the soundscape can be heard.

1. Delete the app, reinstall it from TestFlight and start recording on the
   home screen. Launch the app: the wellness notice appears; dismiss it.
2. Allow Bluetooth when asked.
3. Tap **Scan**: your band and **Simulated headband** are both listed.
4. Tap your band, wait for **Connected**, and show the contact quality.
5. Choose **Focus**, **25 min**, and tap **Start session**.
6. Let the signal check and calibration finish.
7. Show the live score, the scale, the brain rhythms and the soundscape for
   about 30 seconds.
8. Swipe up to the home screen for a few seconds (the soundscape keeps
   playing), then open the app again and show the session still running.
   Don't lock the iPhone: locking stops the screen recording.
9. End the session and show the summary.
10. Open **History**, open the session and **Export** it; show the file in
    Files.
11. Back on Focus, switch to **Meditation** and start it briefly to show the
    calm score, then end it.
12. Open **Settings** and show the soundscape choice and **About**.

Upload the video to YouTube as **Unlisted** (not Private) and check that it
plays in a private browser window before putting the link in the notes.
