Focused Relaxed Mind – Support

Real-time focus and meditation feedback from a BrainBit-family EEG headband (developed with the Mindo band), with a soundscape that guides you.

Getting started

  1. Charge and switch on your headband and put it on.
  2. Open the app, allow Bluetooth, and tap Connect.
  3. Check that all four electrodes show good contact, pick Focus or Meditation and a length, and tap Start session. A short calibration runs first.

No headband?

Turn on Settings › Data › Test mode. A simulated headband then appears in the device list, so you can try every screen.

Common questions

The headband isn't found. Make sure it is on and not connected to another app or phone, and that Bluetooth is allowed for the app in iOS Settings.

Where are exported files? In the Files app, under On My iPhone › Focused Mind.

Is my data uploaded? No. Everything stays on your device. See the privacy policy.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question at github.com/kashubianrodos/focused-mind/issues.
