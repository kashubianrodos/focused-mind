Privacy Policy

Focused Relaxed Mind ("the app", also published as Focused Mind) is a focus and meditation feedback app for BrainBit-family EEG headbands. Last updated: 17 November 2026.

Data the app collects

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to any server.

Data stored on your device

The app reads EEG signals from your headband over Bluetooth and computes a focus or calm score. Session history (scores, brain-rhythm shares, timings), your settings and, only if you turn on Record raw signal, the raw EEG of each session are stored locally on your device. They never leave it unless you export a session yourself as a CSV file, in which case you decide where that file goes.

Deleting a session in the app deletes its data. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth is used only to connect to your headband and receive its signal.

Health information

The focus and calm scores are estimates for personal wellness use. The app is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
